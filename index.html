About Our System

Welcome to the University Facility Booking and Management System, your streamlined solution for reserving and managing campus spaces.

This application enables students and faculty to easily sign up, book lecture halls, laboratories, meeting rooms, or sports facilities, and track their reservations with clear status updates and booking history.

Administrators can efficiently manage user accounts, update facility details, and oversee all bookings across the university.

A new Emergency Booking option now allows users to instantly reserve any available lecture hall slot for the current day without requiring administrator approval, ensuring quick access in urgent situations.

Whether you are a student looking to secure a study space or an admin ensuring resources are well organized, this platform provides a simple, transparent, and efficient way to handle all your university facility needs.
